Outliers — the part nobody quotes

Every question has an answer. The answers cost money.

A better monitor? $250–400
A coach who reviews VOD? $40 / hour
Move closer to a server? $5,000+
A flute that stays in tune? $900
A teacher, weekly, for eleven years? $26,400
Someone free to drive you there? unbuyable

Gladwell’s real argument was never about effort. It was about access — being born at the right time, near the right people, with the right door unlocked.

Bill Gates Lakeside, 1968: a teletype wired to a mainframe when almost no university had one — then seven straight years of programming
The Beatles Hamburg, 1960–62: 270 nights, five or more hours a night — about 1,200 live shows before their first hit

Obsession is the separator. Resources are the ceiling.
